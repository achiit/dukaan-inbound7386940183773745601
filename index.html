Meet your physiotherapist

I opened this clinic so Bijainagar would not have to travel

RK

A few years before I started Aarogya, my grandmother injured her knee. For every session we had to take a bus to Ajmer or Bhilwara, and by the third week she simply stopped going. Her knee never fully recovered. That stayed with me.

In 2021 I opened Aarogya Physio at Krishi Mandi Chauraha, so that farmers, shopkeepers, students and our elders could get proper physiotherapy close to home. I explain every problem in simple Hindi, show you the exercises myself, and check your progress at every visit.

If you are not sure whether physiotherapy will help, just send me a WhatsApp with your problem. I will tell you honestly.

Dr. Rakesh Kumawat

  • The doctor treats you himself, not an assistant, every session.
  • Exercise-based rehab, not just machines. You go home with a written plan.
  • Open 7 days, including Sunday till 4 PM for working patients.
  • Elderly-friendly care with patient handling for those who struggle to walk.
  • Clear fees told upfront, no surprise packages.
